Grexter
Operator of the TMSolo brand · picture frames for Amazon UK & EU

Grexter is a Polish sole proprietorship operated by Grzegorz Zabrzański. We design, source, and sell TMSolo branded picture frames (anti-reflective glass, premium clip-on systems, and decorative formats) through Amazon's UK and European marketplaces. TMSolo is our registered trademark and the brand under which we serve retail customers.

What we do

  • Design and source picture-frame product lines (Anti-reflective P2/P3/P5 series, clip frames, decorative panels).
  • Manage the TMSolo brand on Amazon Brand Registry across UK, DE, FR, IT, ES marketplaces.
  • Operate Amazon Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, and Brand Store advertising for TMSolo.
  • Handle compliance (GPSR, GS1 GTINs, VbG enrollment) and EU-wide FBA fulfilment.
